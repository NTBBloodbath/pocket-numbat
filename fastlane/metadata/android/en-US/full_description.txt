Pocket Numbat is an unofficial, native Android client for the Numbat scientific calculator.

It evaluates Numbat expressions locally using a Rust build of the Numbat engine. No WebView is involved in the calculation path. Units, physical constants, functions, and formatted error diagnostics work offline.

Currency conversion uses the European Central Bank daily reference rates. The app fetches and caches them for offline use; all other math and units work without a network connection.
